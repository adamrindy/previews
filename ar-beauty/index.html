AR Beauty Panel Guide

A beauty-retouching panel for Adobe Photoshop. This is its guide: what every button does, and how to use the ones that need a few steps. Panel v3.8.2.

  1. Clean up: Heal / Clone / Remove Hairs
  2. Skin: FS or Foundation
  3. Dodge & Burn
  4. Face & hair
  5. Color: Adjust
  6. Check layers
  7. Finish & export
One undo each: every button is a single history step named “AR · …”. Paint it in: effects come with a hidden mask, brush selected, white ready. Fade it: lower the group’s opacity. Hover: the panel’s status bar describes each button and shows any error.
